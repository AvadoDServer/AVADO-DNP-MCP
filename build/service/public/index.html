AVADO AI Connect

Let your own AI assistant look after your AVADO

Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor or another assistant to this box. It can check how the box is doing, read app logs and explain problems in plain words. You decide, per assistant, whether it may also restart, install or update apps.

  • It can never see your validator keys or their passwords, wallets or recovery phrases, or your Admin password.
  • It can never change withdrawal or fee settings, remove apps or data, or run commands on the box.
  • Logs are filtered: anything that looks like a key, password or recovery phrase is hidden before your assistant sees it.

Choose a PIN for this page

This page makes the keys your assistants use. A PIN makes sure only you can make or remove them, give an assistant more rights, or read what your assistants did. Choose at least 6 digits you will remember. Your assistants never need this PIN.

Forgot your PIN?
  1. Open the AVADO Admin (my.ava.do), go to AI Connect and press Restart.
  2. Come back to this page within 10 minutes. A “Reset the PIN” button appears here.

Resetting removes all your connections: their keys stop working and you connect your assistants again. Your box, apps and data are not touched. This way nobody else can use a reset to reach your assistants.

ChatGPT, and Claude on the web, run on the internet and cannot reach your home network: for them, switch on “Share AI Connect on the internet” in the Tailscale app. That gives AI Connect a public address; only someone with one of your keys can use it, and the key page you are reading now is never shared.
