AVADO AI Connect

Let your own AI assistant look after your AVADO

Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor or another assistant to this box. It can check how the box is doing, read app logs and explain problems in plain words. You decide, per assistant, whether it may also restart, install or update apps.

  • It can never see your validator keys or their passwords, wallets or recovery phrases, or your Admin password.
  • It can never change withdrawal or fee settings, remove apps or data, or run commands on the box.
  • Logs are filtered: anything that looks like a key, password or recovery phrase is hidden before your assistant sees it.
